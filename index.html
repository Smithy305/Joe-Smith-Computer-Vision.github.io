👨‍🎓 About Me

Machine Learning Engineer with a PhD in Computer Vision and over five years of experience designing, training and evaluating deep learning systems for industry partners including Roku, Procter & Gamble and the German Aerospace Center (DLR). I am comfortable across the full loop, from dataset generation, experiment design and metric selection to production-quality engineering with CI/CD and experiment tracking.

My work spans robust and explainable deep learning, domain adaptation, synthetic-to-real generalisation, learned embeddings for picture-quality optimisation, and multimodal search with vision-language models. I have published research at CVPR and ECCV, including an ECCV Best Paper Award, and I am looking for roles that combine applied research with building and shipping ML systems.

  • Best Paper Award - ECCV VISION Workshop 2024

🛠️ Projects

  • SpottySearch - Visual Search App in Production at a Pottery Café

    Built and deployed a search app now in live use at a real paint-your-own pottery café: staff photograph pieces before firing and find them later from a plain-text description.

    The pipeline captions photos with a vision-language model, embeds them for vector search (pgvector), and ranks matches by similarity; benchmarked VLM caption-then-embed against CLIP and a local Qwen2.5-VL, improving recall@1 from 0.45 to 0.60.

    Full-stack delivery: TypeScript edge functions on Supabase/PostgreSQL, a mobile PWA, GitHub Actions and Netlify.
